01 — Vision

What FirstReport Is

Most Indians who face a legal problem do not know where to start. They cannot afford a consultation just to understand their situation. FirstReport removes this barrier — it is a guided, AI-powered legal journey that starts with a problem statement and ends with either a ready-to-use letter/document or a warm handoff to a vetted lawyer. Everything happens in the user's preferred language.

Core Promise

"You come with a problem. You leave with a plan, a document, or a lawyer — in your language."

🌐 Multilingual First

Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati + English. Language chosen at entry, persists throughout the journey.

⚖️ 7 Legal Domains

Property, Family, Consumer, Employment, Criminal, Business/Contract, RTI & Govt Processes — at launch.

🔒 Auth + Guest Mode

Google SSO for journey history, ratings, and saved documents. Guests get full functionality, no persistence.

02 — User Journey

The 8-Stage Legal Journey

Stage 1
Landing & Language Selection

User lands on homepage. Selects their language from a visual language picker (flag + script name). Language is stored in session/profile. Guest users proceed immediately; a soft "Sign in to save progress" nudge is shown.

Stage 2
Problem Input

Free-text field in chosen language. User describes their situation in plain words. A voice input option (via Web Speech API) is available for low-literacy users. A "Not sure how to describe? Try these examples" tooltip gives domain prompts.

Stage 3
AI Legal Overview

Claude reads the problem, classifies it into a legal domain, and responds in the user's language with: (a) what this situation is legally called, (b) applicable laws or acts, (c) whether this is civil, criminal, or administrative, (d) urgency indicator — "Is this time-sensitive?" with reason.

Stage 4
Documentation Checklist

AI generates a checklist of documents required for this specific legal situation. Each item has: document name (translated), why it is needed, and where to get it. User can check items off. This checklist is saved for logged-in users.

Stage 5 (Optional)
Document Upload & Verification

User may upload documents (PDF, JPG). AI reviews them — checks if the document appears to be the right type, flags obvious issues (expired date, wrong party name, missing signatures). User can skip this and declare "I have all verified documents." A visual progress indicator shows verification status per document.

Stage 6
Action Plan Generation

The full picture: AI decides the resolution path — Court, Government Office, or Out-of-Court. Then provides: step-by-step process in plain language, what type of lawyer is needed (if court), which government office / official to approach (if administrative), what to hand over and in what order, any deadlines or hearing types to be aware of.

Stage 7
Letter / Document Generation

If a formal letter is needed (legal notice, RTI application, complaint to authority, consumer forum letter), AI asks the user if they want it generated. If yes — AI asks 3–5 targeted questions (name, date, opposing party, specific relief sought), then generates a properly formatted, legally worded letter. User can download it as PDF.

Stage 8
Lawyer Marketplace & Exit

User is shown a curated list of partner lawyers filtered by: legal domain, city/state, language spoken, and consultation fee. Lawyer cards show photo, specialization, years of experience, rating (from logged-in users), and a blurred phone number that reveals on click (or after mini payment). User may exit with their documents or book a consultation.

03 — Feature Specification

Complete Feature Set

Authentication & User Management

  • Google OAuth via Supabase Auth — one-click sign in
  • Guest mode — full journey, no account required, no persistence
  • Session continuity — logged-in users can pause and resume a journey
  • Journey history dashboard — list of past journeys with status, date, domain
  • Per-journey document storage (Supabase Storage, private bucket per user)

AI Engine (Claude)

  • Domain classifier — detects legal vertical from free-text problem statement
  • Multilingual response — responds in same language user writes in (detected via langdetect or explicit selection)
  • Document checklist generator — domain-specific, contextually aware
  • Document OCR & verification — uses Claude Vision to check uploaded images/PDFs
  • Action plan generator — court vs. administrative vs. out-of-court routing
  • Letter generator — structured questions → formatted legal letter (RTI, consumer complaint, legal notice, etc.)
  • Streaming responses — word-by-word output for perceived speed
  • Conversation memory within journey — all stages share context

Lawyer Marketplace

  • Dummy lawyers at MVP (10–15 profiles per domain) — Indian photos, realistic bios
  • Filters: domain, state, language, consultation fee range
  • Reveal-on-intent phone number (click to reveal OR micro-payment gate)
  • Post-consultation rating system for logged-in users (1–5 stars + written review)
  • Future: real lawyer onboarding with verification badge

Letter & Document Module

  • Templates: RTI Application, Legal Notice, Consumer Forum Complaint, Police Complaint, Employment Termination Response, Rent Dispute Notice
  • Dynamic question flow based on letter type
  • Generated letter shown in preview with proper formatting
  • Download as PDF (via html2pdf or Puppeteer serverless)
  • Saved to user's journey history if logged in
04 — Technical Architecture

Stack & System Design

Frontend

  • Next.js 14 (App Router) — SSR + streaming support
  • Tailwind CSS — utility-first styling
  • Shadcn/ui — accessible component base
  • React Context + Zustand — journey state management
  • next-i18n-router — language routing (/hi, /ta, /en)
  • Web Speech API — voice input for problem statement

Backend / API

  • Next.js API Routes — all AI calls server-side (no key exposure)
  • Supabase — Postgres DB, Auth, Storage, Realtime
  • Anthropic Claude API — core AI engine
  • Claude Vision — document verification (Stage 5)
  • Vercel — deployment, Edge Functions for streaming
  • Resend — email notifications (journey saved, lawyer contacted)

Database Schema (Key Tables)

users — id, email, name, avatar_url, preferred_language, created_at
journeys — id, user_id, title, domain, language, status, created_at, updated_at
journey_stages — id, journey_id, stage_num, input, ai_output, completed_at
documents — id, journey_id, user_id, file_url, doc_type, verification_status
generated_letters — id, journey_id, letter_type, content, pdf_url, created_at
lawyers — id, name, photo_url, domain[], state, languages[], fee, rating, phone_hash
lawyer_ratings — id, lawyer_id, user_id, stars, review, created_at

AI Prompt Architecture

Multi-Stage Prompt Design

Each stage gets a dedicated system prompt with: (1) role definition, (2) language instruction, (3) domain context injected from stage 3 onwards, (4) conversation history from prior stages, (5) output format specification (JSON for structured data, Markdown for display text). All prompts are stored as versioned templates in /lib/prompts.

06 — Suggestions to Make This Kickass

Recommendations & Power Features

🎯 Suggestion 1: "FirstScore" — Legal Strength Indicator

After Stage 3, show the user a visual score (e.g., 72/100) representing how strong their legal position is based on the described facts. This is extremely engaging, shareable, and helps users understand their standing before investing time/money. Powered by Claude reasoning.

💬 Suggestion 2: Hinglish / Code-Switch Support

Indians naturally mix Hindi and English. Train the prompt to accept and respond to Hinglish — "Mere landlord ne mera deposit nahi diya" should work as naturally as pure Hindi or pure English. This massively increases adoption.

📱 Suggestion 3: WhatsApp Companion

After completing a journey, offer to send the action plan + letter to WhatsApp. Use WhatsApp Business API. This is how most Indians share and store important documents. Huge differentiator.

🔔 Suggestion 4: Court Date / Deadline Tracker

If the AI detects dates (filing deadline, limitation period), create a reminder card the user can add to their Google Calendar via a one-click OAuth flow. Prevents cases from being dismissed for missed deadlines.

📊 Suggestion 5: Anonymous Case Insights

Aggregate anonymised data across journeys to show stats like: "82% of landlord deposit disputes in Delhi are resolved without court." This builds trust, educates users, and gives you a unique data moat over time.

⚡ Suggestion 6: Express Mode (2-Minute Summary)

For returning users or impatient users — "Just tell me what to do" button that skips the educational content and jumps straight to the action plan. Respects user time and increases completion rate.

Legal Disclaimer Strategy

Add a persistent, tasteful disclaimer: "NyaAI provides legal information, not legal advice. Consult a qualified lawyer for your specific situation." Display this at Stage 3 and on every generated letter. This is non-negotiable for legal liability protection.
